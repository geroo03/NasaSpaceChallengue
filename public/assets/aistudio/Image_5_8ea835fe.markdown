## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for agro-tech, precision agronomy, and land-management platforms. It balances technical satellite intelligence with the organic tactile character of sustainable farming. The aesthetic merges **Modern Organic Minimalism** with subtle utilitarian field-ledger accents. 

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Terroir-Driven & Grounded:** Authentic, connected to nature, evoking healthy soils, foliage, and sunlight without feeling rustic or unpolished.
- **Accurate & Analytical:** Technical clarity derived from satellite telemetry, moisture indices, and crop timeline rotations.
- **Warm & Trustworthy:** Friendly, respectful, and dignified, eschewing cold corporate grays in favor of deep vegetal tones, rich terracotta, and soothing cream canvases.

### Design Principles
1. **Natural Pigmentation Hierarchy:** Colors mirror direct agricultural reality (canopy greens, clay terracottas, wheat sands, and deep loam browns).
2. **Deliberate Typographic Tension:** High-contrast pairing between airy, expansive Extra Light headers and dense, grounded Bold functional labels using a single versatile family.
3. **Structured Tactility:** Solid, unpretentious surfaces with organic borders, warm tinted shadows, and pill-shaped telemetry badges.

## Colors

The palette directly adopts the ten-tone 'Earthy Harmony' system. The interface maintains a light, cream-grounded environment where rich vegetative greens and terracotta accents create high functional contrast and semantic clarity.

### The Earthy Harmony Palette & Functional Mapping

1. **Crema (`#FEFAE0`):** Base canvas and global page background. Provides warmth, reduces eye strain under outdoor sunlight, and eliminates stark digital white glare.
2. **Verde Bosque (`#283618`):** Deep brand anchor. Used for main page titles, global headers, primary top-level sections, dark footers, secondary operational action buttons (e.g., "Simular rotación"), and satellite primary iconography.
3. **Verde Oliva (`#606C38`):** Mid-tone vegetative green for interactive elements, navigation active states, secondary buttons, orbit telemetry iconography, and active status indicators.
4. **Verde Salvia (`#A3B18A`):** Soft foliage tint used for decorative accents, subtle borders, hydrological/water indicators, secondary metric charts, and cross-hatch fill patterns in calendar stages.
5. **Verde Niebla (`#E3EBDD`):** Light, restorative container background. Applied to soft cards, section highlights, satellite source pills, and elevated surface tiers.
6. **Arena (`#EFE3C2`):** Primary card fill, structural content borders, input field containers, outline button backgrounds, and fallow stage badges.
7. **Terracota (`#BC6C25`):** Primary action color. Reserved for pivotal call-to-actions ("Guardar lote", "Ver evidencia"), grain crop tags, and milestone alerts.
8. **Terracota Oscuro (`#9A5518`):** High-contrast interactive state. Applied to text links, primary button hover/pressed states, field diary badges ("Cuaderno de campo"), and severe field alerts.
9. **Ocre (`#DDA15E`):** Warm solar accent for intermediate warnings, climate risk telemetry, moisture deficit flags, and calendar fallow droplets.
10. **Marrón Tierra (`#3B2F20`):** Primary reading typography, body copy, tabular data, field measurements, and critical numeric stats ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against Crema, Arena, and Verde Niebla.

## Typography

The typography system relies entirely on **Raleway**, leveraging three distinct weights to establish strict hierarchy, editorial elegance, and structural authority.

### Weight System & Implementation Rules
- **Extra Light (200):** Exclusively designated for large display titles, section openers, and conceptual banners (e.g., "NUESTRA HISTORIA", "PALETA DE COLORES"). Always set with uppercase or title casing and generous letter spacing (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to preserve architectural breathability.
- **Bold (700):** Applied to subheadings, component action labels, tabular numbers, metric values, navigation tabs, and field identification headers (e.g., "Del campo a tu mesa", "MZ-T Maíz tardío"). Delivers density and crisp visual grounding.
- **Regular (400):** Reserved for informational copy, paragraph text, narrative descriptions, timestamps, and auxiliary form labels. Offers high sustained legibility using standard tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface employs a balanced fluid-grid layout bounded inside maximum readable enclosures (1280px for standard analytical dashboards; 1440px for broad field satellite viewers).

### Grid Configuration
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid, `gutter`: 1.5rem (24px), outer canvas `margin`: 2.5rem (40px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `gutter`: 1rem (16px), outer canvas `margin`: 1.5rem (24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-mobile`: 1rem (16px), outer canvas `margin-mobile`: 1rem (16px). All horizontal pill groups and timeline segments convert to side-scrolling horizontal ribbons with kinetic momentum.

### Rhythmic Padding
- Component interior padding adheres to the `space-*` scale: input elements and buttons use `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal; card surfaces utilize `space-lg` (compact) to `space-xl` (hero analytical cards).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer stacking** combined with **warm, diffuse earth shadows**. Rather than cold synthetic blurs, surfaces derive separation through organic pigment steps.

### Layer Tiers
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Crema (`#FEFAE0`), flat.
- **Soft Section Container (Level 1):** Verde Niebla (`#E3EBDD`) or Arena (`#EFE3C2`), 0px shadow, distinct 1px border colored with `#DDCFA9` (soft sand shade) or `#A3B18A` (salvia outline).
- **Card & Active Panels (Level 2):** Elevated via `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(59, 47, 32, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(59, 47, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Modals, Floating Toolbars & Crop Calendars (Level 3):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(59, 47, 32, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(59, 47, 32, 0.08)`.

Outlines throughout the system remain warm and subtle, avoiding cold monochrome borders in favor of muted plant and earth pigments.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture pairs structural stability with softened, tactile edges (Level 2 Roundedness).

- **Cards, Panels & Data Containers:** `border-radius: 0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px), delivering comfortable ergonomic containment for complex agronomic data.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Timeline segments):** `border-radius: 0.5rem` (8px) for buttons and inputs; calendar segments use `0.5rem` to keep adjacent blocks cohesive.
- **Data Source Chips & Status Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`) to immediately differentiate metadata tags and telemetry sources from actionable cards.
- **Icon Tiles:** Soft rounded squares (`border-radius: 0.875rem` or 14px) providing a dedicated thematic backdrop for telemetry graphics.
